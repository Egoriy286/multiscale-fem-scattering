# Rules for this paper directory

Read README.md and AUDIT.md before revising the manuscript. Preserve the prior
paper and all source experimental records. Use only measured, traceable numbers.
Keep shared, static-low, and frozen extrapolation experiments distinct.

Do not silently change PDE signs, field denominators, POD weighting, frequency
splits, or the meaning of a decoder floor. Do not infer new-medium generalization,
multi-seed uncertainty, universal architecture rankings, or speedups from the
present records. Do not invent author affiliations or contact information.

Use the scripts for numerical assets and tables. A text-only edit does not
authorize retraining, generating new labels, overwriting existing runs, or
publishing externally. Compile, check references and overflow, and visually
inspect every final PDF page. Keep all changes local unless requested otherwise.
